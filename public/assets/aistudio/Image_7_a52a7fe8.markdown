## Brand & Style
This design system embodies the understated power, heritage precision, and uncompromising materiality of a bespoke gentleman's tailor and fine jewelry atelier. It balances the tactile gravity of physical luxury retail with the fluid operational speed required of modern Point-of-Sale (POS) touch interfaces and administrative back-offices.

### Persona & Aesthetic
- **Character:** Aristocratic yet utilitarian, confident, surgically precise, and warm-blooded.
- **Visual Style:** Neo-Classical Modernism. High-contrast surfaces pair architectural restraint with warm metallic accents. Interfaces prioritize clean lines, rich contrast ratios, and deliberate negative space over decorative clutter.
- **Atmosphere:** Deep charcoals evoke flannel suiting, velvet trays, and architectural blackened steel, while warm gold details simulate burnished brass hardware, hallmarked bullion, and bespoke monograms.
- **Physical Usability:** Tailored for heavy multi-touch screen use (countertop iPads, all-in-one register terminals) alongside desktop dense-data operational views. Generous tap boundaries and distinct feedback signals ensure fast, error-free checkout during high-value client transactions.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a dark-mode foundation to simulate ambient salon lighting, reduce optical fatigue in high-contrast retail spaces, and spotlight product photography (fine gem facets, worsted wool weaves, precious metals).

### Functional Palette Mapping
- **Canvas Base (`#0F172A`):** The primary environment substrate. Deep, architectural midnight charcoal providing extreme contrast against interactive overlays.
- **Surface Elevation (`#1E293B`):** Structural panels, side sheets, modal backdrops, and POS cart containers.
- **Surface Elevated Interactive (`#334155`):** Sub-cards, quick-key buttons, selected states, and divider lines.
- **Primary Accent (`#D97706`):** Warm burnished gold. Reserved for critical transactional catalysts: "Charge Terminal", "Complete Ring Sizing", primary CTAs, and active order totals.
- **Secondary Accent (`#F59E0B`):** Polished gold highlight. Used for active state indicators, hover highlights, and premium VIP client badges.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#B45309`):** Antique gold. Applied to borders of selected physical inventory, focused inputs, and metric highlights.
- **Text & Contrast Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` & `#F8FAFC`):** Crisp off-white card backgrounds within receipt views, diamond certificate overlays, and high-legibility numerical readouts.
- **Slate Borders (`#334155` dark / `#E2E8F0` light modules):** Razor-thin structural delineation ensuring tactile division without visual noise.

## Typography
Typography reflects the contrast between bespoke, traditional sartorial craft and swift digital utility:

- **Editorial Serifs (EB Garamond):** Used exclusively for high-level anchors—client names, atelier category headers, garment collection names, and receipt titles. Conveys bespoke tailoring heritage and jeweler hallmarks.
- **Precision Modernist Sans (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Governs all transactional, numerical, tabular, and touch-screen microcopy. Chosen for its open apertures, tall x-height, and superior legibility under varying terminal viewing angles and lighting conditions.
- **Monetary Figures & Sizing Metrics:** Numbers utilize `tabular-nums` formatting across both inventory tables and the active register screen to prevent layout shifts during live cart recalculations.
- **Labels & Tags:** Uppercase transforms combined with deliberate tracking (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) create refined, brass-stamped metadata tags for ring gauges, karat ratings, and garment alterations.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout architecture separates POS interactions from administrative data manipulation through distinct ergonomic paradigms.

### Touch Point-of-Sale (POS) Screen Architecture
- **Split Canvas Mode:** 60/40 viewport split on standard counter displays (1024px and above).
  - **Left Area (Catalog Grid):** Multi-touch fluid 3-to-4 column product matrix optimized for rapid finger-tapping.
  - **Right Area (The Docket / Cart):** Fixed 380px to 440px wide column featuring pinned bottom payment triggers.
- **Touch-First Touch Targets:** Minimum button height on register screens is strictly 48px, with standard primary actions scaling to 56px to eliminate mis-taps during rush hours.

### Admin & Inventory Matrix Architecture
- **12-Column Responsive Fluid Grid:** Accommodates dense operational dashboards, bespoke customer alteration histories, ring vault status, and financial reporting.
- **Information Density:** Modulates to a compact vertical cadence in data tables (`space-sm` row padding) while maintaining generous breathing room (`space-xl`) around high-ticket product showcases and executive analytics summaries.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is expressed through structural tonal separation and hairline metallic borders rather than cloud-like shadows.

### Tonal Hierarchy
- **Base Level (`#0F172A`):** Foundation canvas hosting side navigation, global headers, and non-actionable backdrops.
- **Layer 1 Surface (`#1E293B`):** Product catalog tiles, analytical summary cards, and administrative data tables. Rimmed with 1px `#334155`.
- **Layer 2 High-Focus (`#283548`):** Active cart drawer, customer sizing detail side-panels, and ring vault drawer modals.
- **Layer 3 Overlays & Flyouts:** Floating keypad modals, ring sizer selection overlays, and quick-action sheets. Outlined in 1px warm gold (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.45)`).

### Shadow Language
- **Soft Ambient Occlusion:** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`. Applied solely to elevated flyouts and modals to distinguish foreground tasks from transactional POS states.
- **Metallic Edge Highlight:** A 1px top interior line (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) is cast across primary buttons and cards to mimic the beveled bevel edge of fine jewelry mountings.

## Shapes
Geometry is disciplined, tailored, and architectural. The system adopts Level 1 (`0.25rem` / `4px` baseline) roundedness to maintain the crisp silhouette of custom tailoring patterns and geometric gemstone cuts.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Gives an intentional, cut-stone presence with softened edges that feel modern rather than sharp or unyielding.
- **Structural Containers (Cards, POS Grid Items, Flyouts):** `8px` (`0.5rem` / `rounded-lg`). Provides cohesion across large interactive modules.
- **Status Indicators & Swatches:** Pure circular radiuses (`9999px`) are reserved only for ring metal swatches (Platinum, 18k Yellow Gold, Rose Gold, Titanium) and client profile avatars.

## Components

### 1. Action Buttons
- **Primary Register Button ("Complete Sale", "Charge"):** Deep burnished gold background (`#D97706`), contrasting charcoal text (`#0F172A`), uppercase `label-lg` typography, height `56px`. Inner top highlight creates a subtle bevel effect.
- **Secondary Actions ("Add Custom Alteration", "Hold Order"):** Charcoal surface (`#1E293B`), 1px slate border (`#334155`), warm gold text (`#F59E0B`). Height `48px`.
- **Destructive/Void Action:** Low-saturation crimson surface with muted text, converting to high-contrast red only on deliberate two-step press.

### 2. Product Catalog Tiles (POS Grid)
- **Dimensions & Bounds:** Minimum 140px × 140px square touch tiles.
- **Visual Anatomy:** Dark slate substrate (`#1E293B`) framed by 1px border (`#334155`). Product image centered, capped with a top-right precious metal or fabric badge.
- **Price Tag Presentation:** Bold, prominent `title-md` off-white typography (`#F8FAFC`) with tabular currency glyphs.
- **Tap Feedback:** Dynamic micro-scale transition (`scale(0.98)`) and gold border flash (`#D97706`) providing physical confirmation under finger contact.

### 3. Inventory Badges & Attribute Chips
- **Ring Sizing & Metal Hallmarks:** Compact pill chips (`0.25rem` radius) utilizing dark neutral fills with metallic text (e.g., "Size 10.5", "18K Gold", "VVS1 Diamond").
- **Stock Depletion Indicators:**
  - *In Vault / Available:* Border `#334155`, text `#94A3B8`.
  - *Low Stock / Atelier Reserved:* Warm amber accent fill at 15% opacity, text `#F59E0B`.
  - *Bespoke Only / Made-to-Order:* Deep indigo/slate wash.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **POS Quick-Search:** High-visibility text field, height `52px`, dark interior (`#0F172A`), persistent 1px border (`#334155`). Focus ring transitions smoothly to 1px burnished gold (`#D97706`).
- **Barcode & SKU Reader Field:** Distinctive monospaced font integration, persistent right-aligned scan icon, quick-clear tactile touch icon.

### 5. Point-of-Sale Cart (The Active Docket)
- **Itemized Lines:** Clean list entries featuring suit alterations details (e.g., "Hem: +1.25 in", "Sleeve: Bespoke Surge") and ring custom attributes nested beneath line item headers.
- **Price & Quantity Steppers:** Integrated 40px × 40px touch increment/decrement counters designed to eliminate keystrokes for repeated items.
- **Order Ledger Summary:** High-contrast subtotal, luxury tax, bespoke service fee, and active grand total set in `price-display` styling.

### 6. Admin Data Tables & Vault Sheets
- **Structure:** Crisp, low-elevation tables with deep charcoal alternating rows (`#0F172A` / `#141E33`).
- **Headers:** Uppercase tracked `label-sm` in slate gray (`#94A3B8`), border-bottom 2px `#334155`.
- **Row Interactions:** Single-tap slide-out drawer revealing client purchase history, ring measurement profiles, and alteration tailor notes.
